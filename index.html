Liqwy Factory

Liqwy Factory is a private tool run by its owner on their own computer. It publishes videos the owner has already finished to YouTube channels the owner controls.

What it does

  • Uploads a batch of finished videos through the YouTube Data API.
  • Sets each video's title, description, thumbnail and captions, and adds it to a playlist.
  • Sets each video to private, unlisted or public, as the owner chooses.

Who it is for

Only the owner uses it. It is not offered as a public service, and there is no sign-up.
